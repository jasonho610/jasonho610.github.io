Research

  • 2025 – present

    MixJudge — independent research with Prof. Yi-Hsuan Yang, NTU

    Translated perceptual vocal-mixing concepts into audio–text supervision across 11 mixing issues, using controlled DSP transformations with applicability checks and graded severity. Trained a language-queryable critic with contrastive and ranking objectives, and built mixing assistance that selects processing options by learned audio–text similarity.

  • 2021 – 2023

    Speech enhancement and ASR — M.S. research, NTNU

    Reformulated the enhancement encoder as a Sinc-convolution with learnable cutoff frequencies and band gains, making frequency selectivity directly inspectable; ablations raised PESQ from 2.91 to 3.10. NAaLOSS separated residual noise from model-generated artifacts with distinct loss terms, improving downstream ASR without retraining.

  • 2023 – 2024

    Personal VAD — NTNU with Realtek Semiconductor

    Proposed personal VAD for own-voice suppression and set the application requirements, data and metrics with the industry partner. Sinc-Extractor prototype raised frame precision by 36.4% and 7% on small and large datasets; a speaker-conditional variant cut parameters by 71.5% while still gaining 9.8% and 1.6%.

  • 2021 – 2023

    Mentoring

    Mentored a junior graduate researcher through speech enhancement and personal VAD, and guided four undergraduate teams with Prof. Jeih-weih Hung — four papers and two College Student Research Scholarships.
