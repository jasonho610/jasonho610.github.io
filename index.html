I work on audio machine learning and signal processing, and I play jazz tenor saxophone.

The two keep meeting. Musicians spend years learning which differences in sound matter. I want audio models to learn the same things, and I want to be able to check whether they really did.

It all started with DSP. Then speech enhancement and ASR. Then Positive Grid, as a DSP engineer. And now MixJudge. I am giving myself to music technology, with musicianship alongside it.

Audio technology

  • 2026 – now

    Independent research, with Prof. Yi-Hsuan Yang at NTU

    MixJudge: a model you can ask, in plain words, what is wrong with a vocal mix. It learns eleven common problems and ranks how badly a mix has each one.

    arXiv:2610.30783 · submitted to ICASSP 2027

  • 2024 – 2026

    DSP Engineer, Positive Grid

    Real-time audio for guitar software and smart amps. Over forty effects for BIAS X, neural amp models squeezed onto a microcontroller, and tone embeddings that let presets move between platforms.

    BIAS X · Reactor · Spark NEO

  • 2021 – 2023

    M.S. research, with Prof. Berlin Chen at NTNU

    Speech enhancement and noise-robust recognition, plus a collaboration with Realtek on spotting the wearer’s own voice. Five papers at ICASSP, Interspeech, MLSP and SLT.

    What Do Neural Networks Listen To? — ICASSP 2024
